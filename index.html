01. _About me

05. _Other Interests

I'm drawn to the overlap between technical precision and creative expression. Whether I'm sculpting clay, framing a photograph, sketching a character, or writing a guitar riff — I approach all of it with the same curiosity I bring to code: iterate, refine, make it feel right.

I'm also deeply interested in how technology can augment creativity — tools that let artists do more, workflows that remove friction, and software that feels invisible so the work can breathe. If it involves making something from scratch, I'm probably interested.
